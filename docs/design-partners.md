# Stacy Design Partner Program

> A one-pager for prospective design partners. Source for the PDF / web version
> sent during recruitment.

## What Stacy is

Stacy is a coordination layer for AI work. It turns the outputs of your tools
and AI agents into **signed Knowledge Objects** with verifiable provenance,
lets you share them with **per-object consent**, and keeps a **tamper-evident
audit trail** of everything. Connect GitHub, Slack, and Jira; run AI tasks over
that context; produce outputs you can actually trust and trace.

## What "design partner" means here

A small, time-boxed commitment to use Stacy on **real** workflows and tell us
what breaks:

- ~45 minutes/week for 4 weeks
- Use Stacy on at least one real, recurring workflow (not synthetic tests)
- A weekly 30-minute call with the team
- Honest, critical feedback — the rougher the edges you find, the better

## What you get

- Free use through v1.0
- A direct line to the team (private channel, fast responses)
- Real influence on the roadmap — your friction points jump the queue
- Optional named case study (only with your explicit sign-off)

## What we ask of you

- **Real workflows only** — synthetic tests don't teach us anything
- Permission to publish **anonymized** feedback and usage patterns
- A **weekly 30-minute** check-in for the 4-week program
- A named point of contact on your side

## Good fit

- A team of 2–20 engineers
- Already using an AI dev tool (Claude Code, Cursor, etc.)
- Has a recurring coordination pain: release readiness, cross-tool status,
  decision/audit trails, agent-output review
